## Our Brand

The Jabil brand is one of our most important assets, so it is essential that we all understand its value and know how to use it. Each interaction with customers, suppliers, employees, investors, media, or the community is an opportunity for us to reinforce a consistent perception of our company.

**Our Vision** | To be the most technologically advanced and trusted manufacturing solutions provider

**Our Purpose** | At Jabil, we make the world a better place with the way we treat each other; extend innovative solutions; give back to our local communities; and respect the environment.

### Our Voice & Editorial Style

#### Our Voice

Our brand voice is active, informative, pragmatic, and humble. We emphasize clarity, relevance, evidence-based content, accessibility, and action-oriented writing while avoiding jargon, complexity, and arrogance.

**Example — General Statement:**
We provide innovative manufacturing solutions that help our customers improve efficiency and reduce costs.

**Example — With Jabil's Voice:**
We partner with customers to design and build solutions that make manufacturing faster, smarter, and more cost-effective.

#### Editorial Style

Jabil follows The Associated Press Stylebook, also known as the AP Style, for content creation. This stylebook provides the guidelines for consistent copy standards across the organization. A digital or physical copy of the stylebook can be purchased for your team's reference. Enterprise Marketing & Communications has also created Content and Writing Guidelines with more detailed instructions for digital content, blogs, and social marketing.

### Logo | Minimum Clear Space

To ensure visibility and impact, maintain the minimum clear space around the logo. No graphic elements should be placed within the clear space. Be sure to leave the proper clear space between the logo and the edge of your paper or item.

**Rule:** Use the height of the "J" as a guide to determine how much clear space is required around the logo.

#### Examples (Do's and Don'ts)

- ❌ Photo is too close to logo → ✅ Maintain proper spacing from imagery
- ❌ Logo is too close to edges of glass door → ✅ Keep clear space from physical edges
- ❌ Logo is too close to edges of notebook → ✅ Ensure adequate margin on merchandise

---

### Logo | Prohibited Treatments

**Do not change or edit the Jabil logo in any way.**

This includes changing colors, adding effects/filters, combining it with additional elements, or rearranging any part of the logo. Do not distort, skew, stack, or apply shadows or perspective changes to the logo.

#### No Other Colors

- ❌ Retired Logo with Green Arrow
- ❌ All Black*
- ❌ All Sky Blue
- ❌ White with Blue Arrow
- ❌ All Jabil Blue
- ❌ Black and Sky Blue
- ❌ Reversed with Sky Blue Text
- ❌ White with Green Arrow

> *A black logo may be used for black and white documents, where color printing is not an option, such as invoices.

#### No Shape Changes

- ❌ Vertically distorted
- ❌ Distorted
- ❌ Horizontally distorted
- ❌ Skewed / italicized
- ❌ Stacked

#### No Effects/Filters

- ❌ Drop shadow
- ❌ Beveled / embossed

#### No Added Elements

- ❌ Logo as part of another identity (e.g., "JABIL HEALTHCARE")
- ❌ Included in a sentence (e.g., "Happy JABILversary")

---

### Logo | Background Colors & Photos

Make sure the logo stands out clearly, with good contrast between the text, arrow, and background. Preferred color combos are on the left side — and the right side shows errors to avoid.

#### Approved Backgrounds

- ✅ Preferred use on Jabil Blue (white logo)
- ✅ Preferred use on White (Jabil Blue logo)
- ✅ Navy — best contrast for digital use (white logo)
- ✅ Background Grey (Jabil Blue logo)
- ✅ All White on Light Blue
- ✅ Dark on Light Grey

#### Backgrounds to Avoid

- ❌ Poor contrast (Sky Blue on dark blue, or similar low-contrast combos)
- ❌ Poor contrast (Jabil Blue on Sky Blue, or Sky Blue text on bright cyan)
- ❌ Busy background (logo placed over detailed photography)

## Partner Logos | Trademark Usage

Jabil must receive written approval from any other entity (including Jabil's customers) prior to using a non-Jabil trademark. Such approval must be secured in all cases, even when the use will be strictly internal. Any use of non-Jabil trademarks that may be seen outside of Jabil should also be reviewed and approved by Legal and the Enterprise Marketing & Communications team. The person leading the effort for the business should ensure that Jabil obtains written approval for Jabil to use non-Jabil trademark(s). In addition to trademark infringement issues, breach of contract issues may arise. All approvals must be in writing and should come from someone who has the authority to grant trademark permission.

## Brand Acquisitions & Transition

When a company is acquired and retains its existing name and identity, the standard brand application is to add "A Jabil Company" beneath the logo. This ensures brand consistency while honoring the acquired company's legacy and recognition in the market.

Please note that exceptions may apply based on the nature of the acquisition. In some cases, the acquired brand may be fully transitioned to the Jabil brand over time, retiring its original identity. These decisions are made with cross-functional input and follow a strategic timeline aligned to business goals.

**Examples of acquired brand treatment:**
- ProcureAbility — a Jabil company
- Mikros Technologies — A JABIL COMPANY
- Retronix — A JABIL COMPANY
- Pharmaceutics International Inc. (PII) — A JABIL COMPANY

---

### Typography | Prohibited Styles

Good typography emphasizes readability, consistency, and clear visual hierarchy. Simple type treatments help structure the flow of content, while variations in font size and weight direct the reader's attention. Proper spacing between lines and letters improves legibility, and thoughtful alignment paired with strong contrast ensures key elements stand out. All of Jabil's templates include these standard formatting practices.

Avoid using any stylized effects including bevel/emboss, drop shadows, distortions, gradients, or other fonts. They reduce the Jabil brand integrity.

**Prohibited:**
- ❌ No unapproved fonts
- ❌ No stretched fonts
- ❌ No bevel or embossing
- ❌ No skewed fonts
- ❌ NO DROPSHADOWS
- ❌ No unapproved colors
- ❌ No italics except where grammatically necessary (Italics should only be used to emphasize words, indicate foreign terms, and highlight titles of creative works)

## Gradient Accents

Gradient accents are a key design element introduced to add movement and visual interest while enhancing content. They are most effective when used to highlight key elements or frame content, helping to guide the viewer's attention. Gradients should always be applied thoughtfully and sparingly — supporting the overall design without overpowering it.

Use only approved gradient colors from the Jabil brand palette, and ensure all designs remain clear, consistent, and easy to read. When used correctly, gradients can elevate your layout while maintaining a clean, professional look.

### Approved Gradient Combinations

| Gradient | From → To |
|---|---|
| 1 | Sky Blue → Leaf Green |
| 2 | Light Blue → Spring Green |
| 3 | Jabil Blue → Sky Blue |
| 4 | Green → Leaf Green |

### Gradient Applications

- **Gradient Icons** — Icons with gradient color fills
- **Gradient Lines and Fills** — Used as accent lines, borders, or background fills in layouts

## Photography

All photography, videos, audio clips and other graphics must be created by a Jabil employee or an approved vendor.

Copyright is the legal right over intellectual property. It encompasses writing, music, film, design, photography and more. Assets found on the internet are not public domain and using them could violate copyright laws. ALL photos must have appropriate permissions or approved licenses to be included in any Jabil materials. Unauthorized use of photography is strictly prohibited and may result in legal or compliance issues.

Whenever possible, please select photos with realistic imagery and colors.

AI-generated photos should always be reviewed for realistic details to ensure accuracy, believability, and alignment with the intended message.

---

### Photography | Prohibited Styles

Photos play a big role in how people see Jabil's brand. Use images that feel real, consistent, and professional to support our message and visual identity.

**Guidance:**
- Make sure photos are clear, high quality, and well-lit
- Show diverse people in real, relatable settings
- Keep the focus clear — avoid cluttered backgrounds and awkward cropping
- Stick to natural colors that relate to the brand color palette

**Avoid:**
- Low-resolution, blurry, or pixelated photos
- Avoid overly bright, saturated, or trendy effects
- Over-edited or trendy effects (i.e. dropshadows, faded edges, odd borders)
- Stretching, distorting images from their original aspect ratio
- Overlays with illustrations, effects or other elements that cover parts of the photo
- Clip art or cartoon-style images (i.e. Disney characters, superheroes, etc.)
- Outdated looks (old tech, fashion, etc.)

---

### Photography | Products

Photograph individual items with the following in mind:

- Items should be clean and well-lit on a white, seamless background
- Items may be silhouetted or shot with a light shadow
- Avoid harsh shadows and heavy reflections
- Represent colors accurately
- When retouching small items, remove dust, specks, and scratches that would not be seen with the naked eye
- Shoot multiple angles for each item
- Provide shots of other variations/configurations where applicable (For instance, we may want to show how an item looks when it is open and closed.)
- Provide detail shots where applicable (device features, surface textures, etc.)
- Provide additional group shots where it makes sense

**Product shot types:** Light Shadow, Silhouette, Detail

---

### Photography | On-Site

- Clean and tidy the area before shooting
- Don't photograph customer products without permission
- Follow all environmental health and safety (EHS) rules
- Use creative angles when possible
- Logos on equipment may need to be removed or covered
- For all on-site filming, each employee involved must sign the Model Release Form in advance. Your Regional Communications Leader can provide the forms and assist for any additional questions
- Employees can't wear clothing or hats with other brands' logos or artwork
- No political references or slogans

## Site Branding

All site buildings with retired logos (with green triangles or regional names) — whether they are on signage, in print or on-screen — should be phased out over time, with digital happening immediately.

In prominent signage, such as the building exterior and lobby, it may be possible to update the color of the triangle without changing the entire sign. If a division name appears underneath the Jabil logo, it should be removed if/when possible.

## Vehicle Branding

### Passenger Vehicles

Jabil branding can be tailored to work with different vehicles, various paint tones (light versus dark), and available clear space. Because vehicle design varies, care should be taken in the placement and size of the graphics ensure the logo isn't crowded. If the logo must span across two panels on the vehicle, ensure design integrity is maintained.

**Logo usage by vehicle color:**
- **Light colored vehicles:** Jabil Blue logo with Sky Blue arrow (CMYK: 100.60.10.53 + 67.2.0.0)
- **Dark colored vehicles:** White logo with Sky Blue arrow (CMYK: 0.0.0.0 + 67.2.0.0)

**Application options:**
- Triangle stream and logo application (side panel)
- Side logo application options
- Rear logo application options

### Vehicle Wraps | Trucks

Truck wraps feature:
- Front cab: Jabil logo centered
- Side panels: JABIL | A MANUFACTURING LEADER — TRUSTED BY THE WORLD'S BIGGEST BRANDS, with Triangle Stream graphic
- Rear: Jabil logo with optional recruitment/messaging content and QR code

## Branded Merchandise

**Only use approved vendors to create branded merchandise.**

Please choose quality items that reflect the Jabil brand.

Blue, navy, or white items are preferred. However, exceptions may be made for black, dark grey or light blue items.

When possible, use a two-color Jabil logo. Always respect our logo's minimum clear space.

Do not order branded merchandise with customer or vendor logos. These resources are for Jabil branded merchandise only.
